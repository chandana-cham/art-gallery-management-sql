# Art Gallery Management Database (SQL)

## Problem Statement
An art gallery needs a structured database to manage artworks, artists, customers, and sales. This project models real-world gallery operations and enables analysis of inventory, artist performance, and revenue.

## Business Objectives
- Track artworks and artists
- Identify sold and unsold artworks
- Analyze artist-wise revenue
- Monitor customer purchases
- Calculate total gallery revenue

## Dataset Overview
- artists: artist details
- artworks: artwork information and availability
- customers: buyer information
- sales: artwork sales transactions

## SQL Concepts Used
- SELECT, WHERE
- JOIN (INNER & LEFT JOIN)
- GROUP BY, HAVING
- Aggregate functions (SUM, COUNT)
- CASE WHEN
- Subqueries

## Key Analysis Performed
- Available vs sold artworks
- Total gallery revenue
- Artist-wise sales revenue
- Unsold inventory detection
- Customer purchase tracking

## Business Insights
- Revenue is concentrated among a few artists
- Some artworks remain unsold, indicating inventory risk
- High-priced artworks successfully sold
- Repeat customer engagement can improve sales

## Outcome
This project helps gallery owners manage inventory efficiently, focus on top-performing artists, and make data-driven sales decisions.
